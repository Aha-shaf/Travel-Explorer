About us

A small team that plans trips properly

Travel Explorer started as a notebook full of routes, prices and hotel recommendations. It is now a travel desk that plans holidays for people who want a good trip without spending weeks comparing websites.

Our mission is simple: honest pricing, comfortable places to stay and someone to call when a flight is delayed. We plan a limited number of trips each month so every guest gets a real person to talk to before and during the journey.
